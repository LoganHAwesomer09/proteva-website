Why we started

It started with someone we love.

After our grandmother was scammed, we couldn't stop thinking about the families facing the same worry. Two brothers, Logan and Kellen, with guidance from our dad, started Proteva to help.

To us, protection means standing beside someone, not taking away their independence. We're building the kind of help we wished our own family had.
