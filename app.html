We believe in protecting people with their family, never behind their back.

Is my loved one's device protected yet?

Not yet. This is a working prototype of the caregiver experience. You can manage family profiles, explore sample activity, and check a message with AI. The device software that automatically detects and blocks threats is still being built.

Does Proteva read private messages?

There is no background message monitoring in this prototype. The scam checker analyzes only text you choose to submit to it. Our device-protection vision is noninvasive: no reading private conversations or hidden surveillance.

What does archiving an item do?

It moves the item from your overview to History. It doesn't change a threat assessment, allow an app, or take action on a device. Follow any suggested next steps before archiving an item that needs your attention.

Can my loved one choose not to use Proteva?

Always. Setup must happen with their knowledge and agreement. A profile here doesn't install anything on their device. Their device, their privacy, their choice.

Can I change my plan?

Subscriptions are not active in this preview. The planned Home, Family, and Shield options are shown on our website. We'll explain pricing clearly before asking you to subscribe.
