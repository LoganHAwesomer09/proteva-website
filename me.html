Someone who loves you wants to make the online world a little less worrying. Your comfort and your choices come first.

Let's be clear about where we are

Proteva is still a prototype. Creating a family profile does not install anything on your phone or computer, and it does not start monitoring or protection.

What we're working toward

Help that stops scams and suspicious links before they cause harm, and explains concerns in words that make sense. Your family can help with the moments that need a conversation.

Your private conversations stay private

Our approach is to protect devices without reading private conversations. The preview's scam checker sees only text someone chooses to submit. Never include passwords or account details.

You're always in control

Talk through Proteva together before using it. You can say no, change your mind, or ask questions at any time.

What if I don't want to use Proteva?

There is no Proteva device agent to uninstall in this preview. You can close the website, and ask your family member to remove your profile from their Family page. For help with your information, email hello@getproteva.com.
